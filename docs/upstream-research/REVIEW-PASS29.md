[Moving-block source](https://github.com/jesse-ai/jesse/blob/aded4b9d51d432d411a8f7fbf45e47572dff6629/jesse/candle_pipelines/moving_block_bootstrap.py): samples consecutive tuples of absolute close/high/low deltas, rebuilds prices and enforces positive bounds. Its multivariate tuple describes fields of one asset, not inherently an all-asset panel. Timestamps and volume are initially copied and volume is not reassigned with sampled price rows. Thus price/volume association need not survive. Own toy illustration: jointly sampling(-return,lowvolume) then(+return,highvolume) differs from retaining high/lowvolume in the original order. The block-length heuristic is a configuration choice, not a transferable optimal horizon or independence guarantee.

[Base pipeline](https://github.com/jesse-ai/jesse/blob/aded4b9d51d432d411a8f7fbf45e47572dff6629/jesse/candle_pipelines/base_candles.py) rebuilds an internal batch at boundary indices and carries the previous generated close. [Caller](https://github.com/jesse-ai/jesse/blob/aded4b9d51d432d411a8f7fbf45e47572dff6629/jesse/modes/backtest_mode.py), _prepare_routes1328–1405, constructs a separate pipeline per exchange/symbol for trading and data routes using the same kwargs. Same-seed moving-block instances CAN choose identical indices if lengths/batch calls agree; unseeded or differently advanced instances need not. The code does not itself bind a shared timestamp-indexed multi-asset block plan. This is a conditional caller-contract observation, not a universal claim that Jesse destroys correlations or an executed upstream defect.

[Gaussian source](https://github.com/jesse-ai/jesse/blob/aded4b9d51d432d411a8f7fbf45e47572dff6629/jesse/candle_pipelines/gaussian_resampler.py) draws normal absolute price deltas from summary parameters and copies original volume. It uses the global NumPy random API; its constructor differs from the seeded moving-block constructor. Neither a Gaussian approximation nor many artificial paths establishes calibrated market tails, independent empirical opportunities or future win probability. No Gaussian generator or random optimization imported.
