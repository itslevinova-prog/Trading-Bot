# Review pass 8 — range versus trend hypotheses

Re-read targeted sections of the previously pinned, locally archived sources. This is not a completed review of entire repositories; the existing count of 41 unique selected archive files including five licence files is unchanged.

* OctoBot-Tentacles `Evaluator/TA/momentum_evaluator/momentum.py`, `BBMomentumEvaluator.evaluate`: computes 20-period, two-standard-deviation Bollinger bands, returns pending for coincident bands, zero at the middle, signed extreme values outside bands and signed squared relative distances inside. Its callback forwards the construction-data inclusion flag. Those numbers are evaluator notes, not calibrated probabilities or evidence that a lower-band touch will revert; downstream strategy combination determines their use. We did not copy the LGPL implementation or execute the Python stack.
* Freqtrade `freqtrade/templates/sample_strategy.py`, `populate_entry_trend`: the sample long condition combines RSI crossing up, TEMA below the band middle, rising TEMA relative to its preceding row and nonzero volume. This is a concrete example of requiring a recovery condition rather than entering solely because a price is low. It is an example, not a validated profitable strategy. No GPL code was copied.
* Jesse `jesse/indicators/chop.py`: inspected wrapper and signature again. Calculation delegates to `jesse_rust.chop`; the archived wrapper cannot establish its internal numerical implementation or live/backtest equivalence. No claim to have verified or ported that kernel.

Own integration: `context/range-regime.ts` distinguishes repeated alternating close visits from one V-shaped reversal, wick-only touches and repeated adjacent candles at one boundary. Prior 20 bars alone establish bounds, path efficiency and an explicitly short-window ATR estimate; the signal is excluded from those definitions. A closed lower-boundary reclaim proposes a research family only. `strategy/range-entry.ts` prices a conditional setup using existing own ATR and paperPayoff functions. It rejects structurally unaffordable stops and insufficient after-cost reward rather than expanding a target beyond observed resistance. No new order-capable provider or deployed experiment is added.

The fixed definitions (quarters, four boundary transitions, prior efficiency <=0.2, one-ATR support buffer, two-ATR volatility distance, 1.5 conditional net reward/risk) are unvalidated hypotheses registered before the descriptive audit, without parameter search. They are not a statistical stationarity test. Shared windows and assets are dependent. A future complete policy must still retain existing fully warmed volatility checks, portfolio/covariance limits, liquidity/depth, causal ownership/entry provenance and hard broker validation. Original entry thesis must be recorded at entry rather than reconstructed retrospectively.
