# Closed-window range research

Own, non-executable research components distinguish repeated boundary traversals from a single reversal, adjacent touches, wick-only ranges and confirmed breaks. They reuse the existing closed-window validator, Wilder ATR helper and fee-inclusive paperPayoff arithmetic. Definitions and source hashes were registered before the descriptive audit in range-regime-protocol-v1.json. They are hypotheses, not a stationarity test or calibrated forecast.

Only the preceding 20 closed candles define support/resistance, their outer quarters, close-path efficiency and ATR14 initialized on that short preceding window. Four alternating close transitions and efficiency at most0.2 indicate a repeated-range observation. A signal wick below old support must close back inside the lower quarter, above its own open and prior close. A two-sided sweep is vetoed. Missing, duplicate, malformed or off-grid data stay unknown; future candles cannot change preceding evidence.

rangeReversionSetup is a research calculation, not a DecisionProvider or order. It requires fresh costs/book inputs and enough observed resistance distance. Stops must include at least one prior-window ATR below support and a two-ATR volatility distance with2% floor; stops exceeding the selected maximum are rejected rather than clipped. Target is prior resistance, within existing3%..40% supported distances. Existing own payoff arithmetic requires conditional net reward/risk at least1.5. It cannot establish win probability, expected value, full liquidity, portfolio feasibility or execution. A future integration would need the fully warmed volatility checks and all account, budget, covariance, slot, depth, provenance and broker guards; it must not replace them with this short-window ATR.
