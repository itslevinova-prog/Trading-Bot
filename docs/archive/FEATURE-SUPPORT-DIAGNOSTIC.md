# Clock-aware feature-support diagnostic

The own research helper fits geometric support from the SAMEsource-available known training rows as the frozen common8D/aggressor9D models. It consumes pair/feature/clocks only, never payoff or forecast. Population means/scales are train-only and match the frozen transforms. For each training row, the reference distance is its nearest training neighbour at a DIFFERENT decision clock; all same-clock asset siblings are excluded. The reference mean is an empirical geometric scale, not an independent-sample estimate.

A query's diagnostic is nearest earlier-training Euclidean distance divided by that reference mean. The existing marginalz6check is reported independently. Source-missing inputs, malformed/stale clocks, corrupted snapshots and degenerate zero reference remain unknown. A distance of0is not certainty; a ratio above1is not a probability or a trading rule. No distance threshold, forecast adjustment, new entry rule or predictive fit was introduced.

Twelve tests cover a synthetic joint combination that passes marginalz6but lies far from train support, complete same-clock exclusion, query/future poisoning, zero geometry, sparse/invalid/missing inputs, mature labels, duplicate/minimum scopes, source immutability and snapshot changes.
