# Prospective native activity research bridge

`nativeWsActivityReplayStudy` adds `executedActivity` only to caller-selected Paper research replay frames. It reuses the receipt-qualified native journal/window reader. It is not registered as a live strategy/provider and makes no requests, inference or orders. The base enrichment runs exactly once; its clock is authoritative. Policy, broker callbacks, pre-existing features and market fields are preserved. Existing executed-activity evidence cannot be silently overwritten.

Eligibility requires a unique Binance Paper research frame and causal market route, plus all current 4/16/96 closed-bar source windows. Actual registered public sessions, as-of receipts and exact normalized OHLCV matches are required. Missing source, late arrival, stale boundary, mismatching bars, duplicate frames/routes or fixture-only sessions produce explicit unavailable/null summary. Zero observed executions preserve zero totals and null participation/VWAP, rather than neutral imputed flow. Future candle values are not read. Native provenance retains source hashes, raw/facts hashes, actual session identities and first usability. Shared source clocks are not independent samples.
