# Synchronized multivariate block stress

The new research-only adapter takes a complete aligned closed15minute source panel and ONE explicit block-length/start-index plan. Every asset uses the same source times, so common shocks and same-time cross-asset observations are not independently scrambled. Each sampled bar carries its original base volume and all OHLCratios to its actual predecessor close; opening gaps are retained. Prices are rebased from the last known source close. A flat zero-volume initial bar is an explicitly synthetic predecessor, not an observed market bar.

Input checks enforce unique markets/samequote, exactcontiguoustimestamps, interval15, positive validOHLC/nonnegativefinitevolume, shared finalcutoff and sourcecloses availablebythatcutoff. Missing/foreign/future/unaligned data, invalidplans and floating overflow/underflow failclosed. No padding, neutral volume or invented positive price floor. Canonical hashes bind original sources, retained plan and scenario. Output times follow the sourcecutoff; kind=synthetic-synchronized-block-v1 and actualCaptureEligible=false. No source retrieval clocks or price forecasts are invented.

Within-block source sequences and simultaneous price/volume tuples are retained. Reweighting/resampling blocks can change global covariance, serial dependence, drift and tails. Fixed-price rebasing with sampled base volume does not preserve quote turnover or model executable depth/spread/queues. Synthetic data are not additional independent samples. This does not certify stationarity, calibration, profitable entries or loss protection. The fixed16bar/12block witness plan is an integration scenario, not an optimized/bootstrap confidence procedure.

Integration uses all20already archived January markets, last385bars each (7700sourcebars); the loader verifies normalized rows against original raw pages and sourceprotocol. Reversing identical sampled vectors preserves their empirical covariance to roundoff(maxdifference1.40e-18); this special same-multiset invariant is not a general correlation-preservation guarantee.

This is a currency-PNL linearized variance calculation, not trades, portfolio performance, a future risk probability or guaranteed maximum loss. Strategies, fees, stops, execution guards and active accounts are not modified.
