# Closed-price1R gain-protection hypothesis

This is a separately versioned programmed Paper research provider, not a learned/external AI or native trailing stop. It preserves every prior structure/bearish exit and entry/risk guard. A uniquely owned/scoped/noncompound recorded trend position supplies actual entry fill/fee basis and original stop. One modelled riskR is basis minus hypothetical stop proceeds under known fee/slippage-cap assumptions. A past CLOSED price within48h must show at least1R hypothetical net gain under spread/fee/slip-cap assumptions. A later current fresh bid net mark<=0 then permits an additional full SELL. It never loosens/removes the original stop or guarantees a breakeven fill.

Only close points after actual opening and strictly before the current signal can witness the gain. High/low spikes and future/current-signal-only peaks cannot arm it. A bar's close may be usable after entry even if its open preceded entry; no intrabar extrema are inferred. Selected-grid gaps/duplicates/invalid values, mismatched source/fee/scope/identity, compound entries, unknown original stop or stale current evidence fail closed for the additional rule. A recent verified witness is enough without claiming the whole older holding history was observed; it can expire from the48h window.

The replay adds only a read-only derived witness summary to held-position context. Cost caps are hypothetical marks, not observed past quotes or guaranteed sell slippage. Decisions remain scheduled/delayed and may realize a loss after a gap or fees.
